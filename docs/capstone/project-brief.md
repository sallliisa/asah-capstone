# Kos De Mansion project brief

Asah 2026 · Team A26-CS299

## Business need

The owner records boarding-house payments in a book and keeps track of rent, air-conditioner service, and tax deadlines separately. The owner needs one place to see what is due and confirm which payments are complete.

## Product

The mobile web app manages rooms and tenants, generates recurring activities, and lets the owner record work and payments separately. The owner can attach a receipt, check cash by room and period, and open an activity from an inbox reminder or iPhone notification. Tenant history follows the tenant across room changes. Occupants can report room problems, and the owner can track each report and link it to a repair activity.

Pi answers questions about the boarding house using permitted app records and Indonesian guides. The tenant assessment model ranks records for owner review. The app shows a separate state when the history is too short.

## Demo and evidence

- Use sample room, activity, and payment records. Test the owner’s main tasks on a phone, tablet, and desktop.
- Compare settled cash totals with the source activities. Check monthly rent and four-month service schedules for duplicates.
- Demonstrate the source link in a Pi answer and reopen the conversation. Follow an iPhone notification to its activity.
- Assess the models with the documented data splits. Report synthetic model limits and record the owner’s feedback separately.
